# Classified shape → topic entry

One row per routing decision — a topic entry, or one page inside it: the shape as
reporters phrase it, the owner the row assigns, and the entry or page to open (last
cell). A row's `**or**` clauses belong to the same
topic — they are the variants that were recorded together — and a row that says *not
this topic* is a route away from it. Version pins, reporter confirmations and open
questions inside a row are conditions of that route, not decoration. Bare
`page.md` names inside a row are relative to the topic entry named in that row's last
cell (`references/<topic>/`), so one vocabulary is reused without repeating the path.
