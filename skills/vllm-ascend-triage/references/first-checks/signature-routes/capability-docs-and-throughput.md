Scope: Track D deliverables and perf claims: "does F work on M", per-SoC recipe or quantized-weight availability, document/community-test reports, env-flag retirement sweeps, and reports whose evidence is a profiler table or a throughput number rather than a failure.

Inherit `index.md` and the SKILL.md rules. Rows are in no particular order — scan the left column, and read the middle cell before following the link.
