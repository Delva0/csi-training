# Serving, output, tool-call and accuracy signatures

Scope: The engine is up and the complaint is about a request or an answer: chat-template/tokenizer mode, reasoning routing, tool-call bookkeeping, truncation/early-stop, scoring gaps, garbled or repeated text, and the flags/log routes that appear to do nothing.

Inherit `index.md` and the SKILL.md rules. Rows are in no particular order — scan the left column, and read the middle cell before following the link.
